## Three-frame storyboard

1. **Arrival, 0–2 seconds:** wide three-quarter view of the nested titanium gateway; a cyan signal approaches. Empty dark space on the left supports the introduction.
2. **Inside, 2–5 seconds:** camera gently advances along the signal through the nested portals. Recessed cyan edges imply routing and security; architecture remains coherent.
3. **Scale, 5–8 seconds:** camera clears the portal and lifts slightly to reveal the connected infrastructure beyond, with restrained amber telemetry lights. Settle into a composition that can transition into selected projects.

## Continuous-shot video prompt

Use the supplied architectural hero as the opening frame and preserve its exact materials, geometry and lighting. Create one silent 8-second continuous cinematic camera shot, landscape 16:9. Start with a wide three-quarter view of the nested brushed titanium gateway in a dark graphite architectural environment. A thin cyan signal travels along the floor through the gateway. Slowly dolly forward along the signal, pass through the nested portals, then gently lift to reveal the orderly connected infrastructure beyond, with a few restrained amber telemetry lights. Ease into a quiet final composition. Preserve dark negative space for website copy throughout, especially on the left. No cuts, no rapid acceleration, no camera roll, no geometry morphing, no new objects appearing, no text, logos, interface elements or audio. Keep architectural edges crisp and glow controlled. The motion must remain legible when scrubbed forward and backward by scrolling.

## Integration boundary

Live HTML supplies headings, navigation and calls to action. Generated imagery contains no interface text. This checkpoint creates artwork only; the existing homepage stays available until the cinematic asset and scroll prototype are ready for review.
